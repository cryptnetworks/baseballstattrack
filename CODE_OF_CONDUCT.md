# Code of Conduct

## Our standard

We are committed to a welcoming, respectful, and harassment-free project for everyone, regardless of background or experience.

Constructive disagreement is welcome. Personal attacks, discrimination, harassment, doxxing, sexualized conduct, and deliberate disruption are not.

## Enforcement

Report concerns privately to the repository owner. Maintainers may remove comments, restrict participation, or take other actions needed to keep the project safe.
